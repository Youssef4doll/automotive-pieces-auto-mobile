# This app

Read `BRIEF.md` before writing code. It holds the owner's rules — the binding
one being **only render data that exists**: no invented stock, delivery dates,
compatibility, tracking numbers or contact details. When a field is empty the
screen says less; it never fills the gap with something plausible.

The brand tokens are in `src/constants/theme.ts` and are copied from the
website's `globals.css`. Change them there, in one place, or the two front
doors drift apart.
